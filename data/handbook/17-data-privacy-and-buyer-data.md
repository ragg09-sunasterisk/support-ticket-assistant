# Data privacy and buyer data

## Who controls buyer data

Merchants collect buyer data (name, phone, address, email) to fulfil orders. Under the Philippine
Data Privacy Act, the merchant is the personal information controller and Kariton processes the data
for them.

## Buyer deletion requests

When a buyer asks a merchant to delete their data, the merchant uses
**Customers > (buyer) > Delete personal data**. This:

- removes the buyer's name, phone, email and address from the shop,
- keeps the orders with the amounts, so sales reports stay correct,
- replaces the buyer name on old orders with "Deleted buyer".

Deletion cannot be undone. Orders with an open refund or dispute cannot be anonymized until they
are closed.

## Sharing buyer data

Support never gives a buyer's personal data to anyone through a ticket, including the merchant who
sold to them, when the request is for a purpose other than fulfilling the order. Merchants already
see the details they need on the order page. Requests to reveal a reviewer's identity or contact
details are refused.

## Data requests from authorities

Requests from police, courts or regulators go to Legal. Support does not confirm or deny whether
Kariton holds data about a person.

## Reporting a possible breach

If a ticket suggests buyer data was exposed, for example a merchant sees another shop's orders,
escalate to the Security team immediately as **critical**.

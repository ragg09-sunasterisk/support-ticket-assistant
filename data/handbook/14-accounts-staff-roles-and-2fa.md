# Accounts, staff roles and 2-step verification

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, payouts and transferring ownership. One per shop. |
| Admin | Everything except billing, payouts and ownership. |
| Staff | Permissions chosen by the owner, such as orders only or products only. |

Owners create staff accounts in **Settings > Staff**. A staff member with only the **Orders**
permission can view and update orders but cannot see payouts or edit products and prices.

## 2-step verification

All owners must use 2-step verification. Each owner gets 10 backup codes when they turn it on.

## Locked out without the 2FA device

1. Ask the owner to sign in with a backup code.
2. If they have no backup codes, start identity verification: a selfie with a government ID and a
   matching name on the payout bank account. This takes up to 1 business day.
3. Never disable 2-step verification because a ticket asks for it, and never accept identity proof
   over chat. Suspected account takeover goes to the Security team.

## Transferring ownership

The current owner transfers ownership in **Settings > Account > Transfer ownership**. If the owner
is gone and cannot be reached, the new owner submits a business ownership document and identity
verification, and the Security team approves the change. If the old owner is reachable, the
self-service transfer is always faster.

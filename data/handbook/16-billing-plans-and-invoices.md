# Billing, plans and invoices

## Subscription charges

Subscriptions are charged on the 1st of each month to the card on file. If a charge fails, Kariton
retries on days 3 and 7, then limits the shop to read-only on day 10 until the balance is paid.

## Looks like a double charge

A pending authorization and the final charge can both show on a statement for a few days. If two
**posted** charges appear for the same month, it is a real duplicate: confirm in
**Billing > History** and refund the duplicate from the billing admin. Duplicates are **medium**
severity; the refund reaches the card in 5 to 14 banking days.

## Changing plans

Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle. When
downgrading:

- Staff accounts above the new plan's limit are suspended, not deleted.
- Moving to Starter disconnects the custom domain; the default address keeps working.
- Order history, products and customers are kept on every plan.

## Invoices and official receipts

Every charge has an official receipt in **Billing > Documents**. Merchants add their registered
business name, address and TIN in **Billing > Business details**; receipts issued after that
include them. Older receipts can be reissued once with the new details from the same page.

## Pricing changes

Price changes are announced by email at least 60 days ahead. Support does not discuss prices that
have not been announced.
